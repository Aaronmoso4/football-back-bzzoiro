YOUSINE LIVE STREAMING TV privacy policy

Updated 19 October 2026

YOUSINE LIVE STREAMING TV provides football scores, fixtures, statistics and model predictions. No user account is required.

Data and purposes

The app stores your appearance and privacy preferences and cached football data on your device. Requests to our service include network information such as your IP address. Our service applies request limits and caching to protect API quotas. No device attestation or app verification token is required.

Football requests are relayed to Bzzoiro Sports Data. Provider credentials remain on the server. Server application logs contain response status and request duration; hosting infrastructure may maintain separate operational logs. We do not include provider secrets or app verification tokens in application logs.

Advertising

Google AdMob displays advertisements. The app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to collect privacy choices where required and only requests ads when permitted by that platform. You can revisit advertising privacy options in Settings when available. Advertising services may process device identifiers, IP addresses, interactions and diagnostic data under their own policies.

Optional notifications and diagnostics

Push notifications are off until you enable them. Enabling them uses OneSignal, which processes a push subscription/device identifier and delivery information. You can disable push in the app or Android settings. Optional usage analytics and crash reporting are off by default; enabling them allows Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to process usage and diagnostic information. Disable the option in Settings to stop future collection.

Service providers

Our infrastructure uses Render, Firebase, Google AdMob and OneSignal. These providers may process information in countries outside yours. Their policies describe their own retention and safeguards:

  • Google privacy policy
  • OneSignal privacy policy
  • Render privacy policy

Your choices and requests

Clear local data through Android app settings or uninstall the app. Changing collection settings stops future collection but does not automatically erase records already held by providers. Contact service@us.com to ask about access, deletion, retention or other privacy requests. We handle requests according to applicable requirements and provider capabilities. The service is not directed to children.

Predictions

Predictions are uncertain informational estimates and do not guarantee a result or financial return.
