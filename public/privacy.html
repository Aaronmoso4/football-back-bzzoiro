Privacy Policy for Live Football Scores

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Android app Live Football Scores (package com.sunsetpm.livescores, “the app”) handles, why, and the choices you have. The app shows football scores, live matches, fixtures, league tables, top scorers and head-to-head results. You do not need an account to use it.

Information we do not collect

The app does not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or payment details. We do not use separate analytics tools, and we do not sell your personal information. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, described below.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob: a banner on the Scores screen, small labelled “Ad” cards, and occasional full-screen ads between screens. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use information such as your device’s advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and how you interact with ads.

  • Consent: if you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent using Google’s consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can choose non-personalised ads.
  • Your controls: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone’s settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • How Google uses this information is explained at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in Google’s privacy policy.

Information stored only on your device

The app keeps a few things on your phone so it works the way you set it up:

  • Your preferences: app theme (System, Light or Dark), whether you finished the introduction, your notification choice, and whether the rating card has been shown.
  • Recently loaded football data (scores, fixtures, tables, teams), saved temporarily so the app loads faster and uses less data.

This information stays on your device. You can remove it at any time by clearing the app’s storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

Football data and our server

To show football data, the app connects to our server at football-back-bzzoiro.onrender.com, which is hosted by Render. Like any internet connection, these requests include your device’s IP address and basic technical information (such as which data was requested and when). The server uses this only to answer the request. Its logs record the request path, response status and duration, and are kept by our hosting provider for a limited time for security and troubleshooting.

Our server gets the football data itself from Bzzoiro Sports Data. No information about you or your device is sent to Bzzoiro.

Notifications

The app may ask for permission to show notifications. Allowing them is optional, and you can turn them off at any time in the app’s Settings or in your phone’s settings. Allowing or denying notifications does not send any personal information to us.

Links to other services

  • WhatsApp: the WhatsApp button opens an invitation to our football channel’s WhatsApp group in the WhatsApp app or your browser. Joining is your choice, and anything you share there is covered by WhatsApp’s privacy policy.
  • Google Play: “Rate the App” and some rating choices open the app’s page on Google Play, which is covered by Google’s privacy policy.
  • Sharing: “Share” opens Android’s share menu with a link to the app; nothing is sent until you choose where to share it.

Children

The app is a general sports information app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

All connections between the app and our server use encrypted HTTPS.

Your choices and rights

Because we do not keep personal information about you, there is normally nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have a question or request about your data, contact us at the address below and we will respond as required by the law that applies to you.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
